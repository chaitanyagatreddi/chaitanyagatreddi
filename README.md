# 👋 Hi, I'm Chaitanya Gatreddi

I build GTM agents and small apps for attribution, SEO and AEO, outbound signals, and GTM forecasting. Based in Bangalore.

## 🛠️ What I build

- 📊 **Attribution and funnel tooling:** connecting product events, ads and content to pipeline and revenue.
- 🔎 **SEO and AEO:** SERP-based keyword clustering, crawl and revenue-leak audits, and visibility checks across AI answer engines like Google AI Overviews and Perplexity.
- 📡 **Signal and outbound agents:** finding in-market accounts, contributors and hiring intent from public data, then scoring them with LLMs.
- 📈 **GTM forecasting:** spend, funnel, CRO and LTV/CAC models built on cited benchmarks.

🧰 Stack I keep coming back to: Python, FastAPI and Flask, Browserbase and Playwright, OpenAI and Claude, Trigger.dev, React, and Vercel.
